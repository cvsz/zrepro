## Reference taxonomy
The workflow is informed by the public taxonomy in `wtsxDev/reverse-engineering` while converting tool/resource categories into authorization-aware, evidence-driven workflows. Third-party content is not vendored.

## Completion criteria
Tool completion is not analysis completion. Require artifact identity, reproducible evidence, stated uncertainty, environment/platform context and a direct answer to the authorized question.
